What this is not

The readout is not legal, medical, regulatory, financial, or professional approval. It is not a broad audit of the company. It is a focused review of one piece of work and the material supplied with it.

Common questions

Do you rewrite the work?

The readout tells you what holds up and what does not. Fixing or rewriting it is a separate piece of work.

Can we send sensitive material?

Do not send sensitive material through the website form. Data handling and access are agreed before the work begins.

Is the readout only for documents?

No. Good Remedy can also review one workflow result when the intended result and supporting material are available.

What happens after five business days?

You receive the readout and a short handoff. A larger repair, pilot, or platform deployment is a separate decision.
