About Good Remedy

Common questions

What buyers usually ask first.

What is Good Remedy?

Good Remedy is the operating layer for business AI, delivered through a managed software platform, implementation, and ongoing support.

What does it mean for AI to be part of the business?

It means the AI works from current company records, within defined authority, and with continuity that survives changes in tools and models.

Is Good Remedy an AI agent?

No. Good Remedy sits around the agents, models, applications, records, and people required to run the business operation.

Does Good Remedy replace our existing AI tools?

Usually no. Good Remedy works across the operation and uses the models and systems that fit.

What do we need for the first conversation?

One recurring job, one real example, and the result the business needs. A sanitized example is enough.
