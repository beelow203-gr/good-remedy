Control and custody

Keep AI work under company control.

Good Remedy preserves the business context, decision rights, and operating record around AI assisted work. Your team decides what the operation is, what may move, and who has authority. The work remains available to continue across people, tools, and models.

Formation

Define the purpose, source of truth, decision rights, and operating limits before AI work moves.

Custody

Keep current work, approved changes, results, and history attached to the business so authorized people and systems can continue it.

A sanitized example is enough to begin. Data handling, access, model and tool use, retention, and approved connections are agreed before live work begins.

About Good Remedy

Common questions

What buyers usually ask first.

What is Good Remedy?

Good Remedy is a managed AI operations platform. It combines software, implementation, and ongoing operating support.

How is this different from consulting?

The relationship begins with hands on implementation, but the enduring product is the operating environment and capability the business continues to use.

Can Good Remedy support more than one department?

Yes. The first operation is deliberately narrow. The platform can expand across the business when the result proves useful.

Does Good Remedy replace our existing AI tools?

Usually no. Good Remedy works across the operation and uses the models and systems that fit.

Do we need an internal AI team?

No. Good Remedy is designed to provide much of the operating discipline and support that many companies do not want to build alone.

What do we need for the first conversation?

One recurring job, one real example, and the result the business needs. A sanitized example is enough.
