About Good Remedy

Common questions

What buyers usually ask first.

What is Good Remedy?

Good Remedy is a managed AI operations platform. It combines software, implementation, and ongoing operating support.

How is this different from consulting?

Good Remedy does more than advise. We implement the operation in the platform and can remain responsible for support and ongoing operation.

Can Good Remedy support more than one team?

Yes. The first operation is deliberately focused. The platform can expand across the business as each additional use proves worthwhile.

Does Good Remedy replace our existing AI tools?

Usually no. Good Remedy works across the operation and uses the models and systems that fit.

What do we need for the first conversation?

One recurring job, one real example, and the result the business needs. A sanitized example is enough.
