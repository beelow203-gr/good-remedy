Control and custody

Control how the work is formed. Keep custody of what the work becomes.

Good Remedy keeps AI assisted work organized around the company's purpose and under the company's authority. The work should not disappear into one model, one chat, or one vendor.

Formation

The company decides what the operation is, what matters, what may change, and where human judgment remains decisive.

Custody

The current work, results, corrections, and operating history remain attached to the client operation so another authorized person or system can continue it.

Common questions

What buyers usually ask first.

What is Good Remedy?

Good Remedy is an AI operations company and software platform delivered through managed implementation.

Is this consulting or software?

The first operation is hands on because it has to work in the real business. The enduring product is the operating capability, software environment, support, and managed operation.

Do we need an internal AI team?

No. Good Remedy is meant to provide much of the operating discipline and support that many companies are not going to build alone.

Does Good Remedy replace our existing AI tools?

Usually no. Good Remedy works across the operation and uses the tools that fit.

Do we need live system access for the first conversation?

No. A sanitized example is enough to see whether there is a useful place to start.
