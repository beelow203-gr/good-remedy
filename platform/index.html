Common questions

What the platform is and is not.

Is Good Remedy an AI agent?

No. Good Remedy is the operating layer around the agents, models, records, applications, and people required to run the operation.

Is Good Remedy tied to one model provider?

No. The platform is designed around the business operation so the models and tools can change when the business needs them to.

Does the platform support more than one department?

Yes. The first operation is focused so it can be judged. The platform can expand across the business as additional uses prove valuable.

Is Good Remedy a public self service app?

No. Good Remedy is currently delivered through managed implementation around the operations the client chooses.
