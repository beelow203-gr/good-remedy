Common questions

What the platform is and is not.

Is Good Remedy a public self service app?

No. Good Remedy is currently delivered through a managed implementation around a specific client operation.

Is this a software subscription?

After the client accepts the first operation, the environment can continue under an ongoing software and support agreement.

Does Good Remedy replace our current AI tools?

Usually no. The platform is designed to work with the tools that fit the operation.

Can Good Remedy run in our environment?

Deployment depends on the client, the data, and the systems involved. The private pilot establishes the right boundary before a larger deployment.
