Control and custody

The company should control the formation and keep custody of the work.

Good Remedy keeps the operation outside any single model session or vendor surface. The business retains authority over what the work is and what may happen next.

Common questions

What the platform is and is not.

Is Good Remedy a public self service app?

No. Good Remedy is delivered through managed implementation around the operations the client chooses.

Is this a software subscription?

The relationship can include software, support, and managed operations after the first operation is accepted.

Does Good Remedy replace our current tools?

Usually no. The platform works across the operation and uses the tools that fit.

Can Good Remedy support more than one use case?

Yes. The first operation is narrow so it can be judged. The platform and operating discipline are meant to support wider AI operations over time.
