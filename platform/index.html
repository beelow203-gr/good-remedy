Common questions

What the platform is and is not.

Is Good Remedy a public self service app?

No. Good Remedy is delivered through a managed implementation around the operations the client chooses.

Is this a software subscription?

The ongoing relationship can include the software environment, support, and managed operations after the first operation is accepted.

Does Good Remedy replace our current tools?

Usually no. Good Remedy works across the operation and uses the models and systems that fit.

Can the platform support more than one department?

Yes. The first operation is focused so it can be judged. The platform can expand across the business as additional uses prove valuable.
