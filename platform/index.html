Common questions

What the platform is and is not.

Is Good Remedy a public self service app?

No. Good Remedy is delivered through managed implementation around the operations the client chooses.

Is this a software subscription?

The ongoing relationship can include software, support, and managed operations after the first operation is accepted.

Does Good Remedy replace our current tools?

Usually no. The platform works across the operation and uses the models, applications, and records that fit.

Can it support more than one operation?

Yes. The first operation is narrow so it can be judged. The platform can expand across the business over time.

Can it run in our environment?

Deployment depends on the client, the data, and the systems involved. The pilot establishes the right boundary before a larger deployment.
