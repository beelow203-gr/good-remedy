Common questions

Can you repair an AI agent built by someone else?

Yes, when the current tools and access allow it. Good Remedy can also work beside the original builder.

Do you only fix prompts?

No. The prompt may be fine. The failure may be in the sources, handoff, integration, approval, or even in what the team means by finished.

Do you need production access right away?

No. Start with a sanitized example and the failure people keep seeing.

Will you replace the whole stack?

Not unless the stack is the actual problem. One broken workflow does not justify rebuilding the company.
