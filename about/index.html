Company

Good Remedy comes from operations, not an AI lab.

The company was built around a familiar problem. The tool can help, but somebody still has to remember the real job, catch what is missing, and answer for the result.

Founder

Benjamin Lopez

Benjamin has led healthcare operations, enterprise client work, systems, teams, business development, contracts, rates, and service delivery across Bloomberg, Mount Sinai, BAYADA, and other operating companies.

Those jobs were different, but the operating problem was usually the same. A process could look complete on paper and still depend on one person to remember everything, repair the handoff, and make sure the work actually finished.

That is the part Good Remedy now fixes in AI workflows.

What the company does

Build the workflow. Fix the workflow. Check the work.

Good Remedy works with companies that already have useful AI tools or a clear job they want AI to help perform. The first engagement stays close to one real workflow and one result the client can judge.
