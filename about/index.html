Good Remedy/Company

Company

Good Remedy comes from operations, not an AI lab.

The company was built around a familiar problem. The AI can help, but somebody still has to remember the real job, catch what is missing, and answer for the result.

Founder

Benjamin Lopez

Benjamin has led healthcare operations, enterprise client work, systems, teams, business development, contracts, rates, and service delivery across Bloomberg, Mount Sinai, BAYADA, and other operating companies.

Those jobs were different, but the operating problem was usually the same. A process could look complete on paper and still depend on one person to remember everything, repair the handoff, and make sure the work actually finished.

Good Remedy turns that operating responsibility into a private platform around the work.

What the company is building

A private operating environment for important AI work.

Good Remedy starts with one operation, puts it into real use, and gives the client a closed place to see the current work, the result, the open decisions, and what happens next.
