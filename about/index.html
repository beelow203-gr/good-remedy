Company

Every business will have AI. The question is whether it will answer to the business.

Good Remedy is building the operating layer that grounds AI in the company's records, authority, decisions, and work.

The market shift

Intelligence, voice, and distribution are becoming abundant.

Platforms can give every business models, agents, personalities, and channels. They do not determine which records govern the business, what has changed, who may decide, what the AI may do, or how the work survives a change of model or vendor.

The Good Remedy role

Make business AI properly grounded, answerable, and continuous.

Good Remedy provides the software and operating discipline that connect AI to the company it represents. The business defines the purpose and authority. The operation carries the current work, accepted changes, results, and history.

What the company provides

One operating layer across models, tools, records, and people.

  • AI operations platform
  • Managed pilots and implementation
  • Business grounding and authority
  • Continuity across models and vendors
  • Workflow repair and work review
  • Ongoing support and managed operations

Founder

Good Remedy was founded by Benjamin Lopez after years leading healthcare operations, enterprise client work, systems, service delivery, and business development. The company comes from work that has to remain current, accountable, and usable after the first impressive output.
