The method

Five steps, the same for every operation.

  • Define the result. What the operation is for, who relies on it, and what counts as done.
  • Map the operation. The inputs, the records that govern it, the decisions in it, and who may approve each one.
  • AI prepares, people decide. AI drafts from the governing material only. Anything the material does not establish becomes a question for a named person, never a guess.
  • Record what actually happened. Prepared, approved, sent, and received are kept as separate facts, so nobody mistakes a draft for a delivery.
  • Accept, correct, carry forward. The business judges the result. Accepted corrections stay in the operation for every later cycle.
Illustrative example

Monthly client reporting at a 40 person agency.

The agency sends monthly performance reports to 25 clients. Account leads spend about two days a month assembling them from three systems, and copying mistakes reach clients a few times a quarter.

ElementIn this operation
Intended resultEach client receives an accurate report by the fifth business day, with every figure traceable to its source.
Governing recordsPlatform exports, the contract scope in the CRM, and last month's approved report.
AuthorityAI prepares the draft. The account lead approves it. Anything about pricing or scope goes to the account director.
ExceptionsA figure that moved more than 30 percent, a missing export, or a client request outside scope is flagged for a person, not explained away by AI.
EvidenceEach report carries a short record: sources used, flags raised, who approved it, and when it was sent.
CorrectionWhen a lead corrects a recurring error, the correction becomes part of the operation for every later report.
AcceptanceAfter two cycles the agency compares time spent, errors reaching clients, and whether leads trust the draft, then decides to stop, repair, continue, or expand.

How your material is handled

When you send an example of your work.

  • Use: only for your work. It is not shared, not used in marketing, and not used for anyone else's work.
  • Tools: AI tools (ChatGPT and Claude) are used with the settings that stop them using your material to train their models.
  • Keeping and deleting: an example is kept until you have seen the result, then deleted within 30 days, or sooner if you ask.
  • Sensitive information: leave out health, financial, or other sensitive personal details. For care organizations, this means no patient health information.
  • Your say: ask at any time what Good Remedy holds or to have it deleted.

For paid engagements, data handling, access, and retention are agreed in writing before live work begins.
